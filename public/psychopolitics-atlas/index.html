Field guide · 2026 edition

One field, nine lenses, a register of doubt

A genealogy of the intentional study of density, coercion, persuasion and behavioral engineering — from Calhoun's rodents and the radio demagogues through Cold-War human experimentation to the platform present and the contemporary population-control apparatus. 654 nodes, 1,138 relations, each opening to its record, an evidence tier where triaged, and source notes where recorded. The main arc runs from the 1920s; the dataset also carries antecedents from 1903.

01Documented ≠ effective. Intent and ambition are not demonstrated capability — MK-ULTRA's “mind control,” Cambridge Analytica's psychographics.
02The replication failure. Human crowding never confirmed Calhoun; hierarchy and control predict pathology. Its suppression is part of the politics.
03The loop. Sociology theorizes it, the lab “proves” it, the laundered proof returns to policy and the feed.
