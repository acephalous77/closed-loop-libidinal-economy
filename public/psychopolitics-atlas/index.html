Some 600 nodes, each opening to its full record, evidence tier, and primary sources. Three rules hold it together:

01Documented ≠ effective. Intent and ambition are not demonstrated capability — MK-ULTRA’s ‘mind control,’ Cambridge Analytica’s psychographics.
02The replication failure. Human crowding never confirmed Calhoun; hierarchy and control — not density — predict pathology. Its suppression is part of the politics.
03The loop. Sociology theorizes it, the lab ‘proves’ it, the laundered proof returns to policy and the feed.
