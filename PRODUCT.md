# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, deliberately equal: **researchers and adjacent scholars** (the
closed-loop framework's citers, students, and interlocutors — they arrive
through the apparatus, the argument, and the citation surface) and **the art
world** (curators, editors, galleries, festivals — they arrive through the
seven works). Neither is primary; each is expected to arrive through the
other.

## Product Purpose

autostimulus is the press of A. Selimović: seven works in seven registers —
a critical-theory framework, a bureaucratic agency manual, a product brochure
and zine, a coloring book, an explorable specimen atlas, a relational atlas
of the socialization apparatus, and an essay-descent through a metered life.
The site exists to publish them with fidelity to each work's own voice and to
make the oeuvre legible as a practice.

## Positioning

The site performs the argument its flagship work makes: one honest,
session-local instrument (the reader's apparatus) shows the reader the shape
of being capturable while capturing nothing — no analytics, no storage, no
third-party requests. A neighboring site could copy the layout; it cannot
truthfully copy this posture.

## Operating Context

- The works span decades (2015–2026) and were designed as distinct registers
  (flyer/console, academic apparatus, federal form, brochure, riso zine,
  coloring book, field guide, descent).
- The framework's apparatus is a citable object; numbered results (§ ·
  Lemma · Theorem) are canonical within v2, and the v1 mathematics and
  dynamics remain on file as the 2026 archive.
- Prose published in the author's name passes the de-AI scan and his own
  ruling at the gate before it is final.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Binding, durable constraints (confirmed 2026-10-10):

1. **No third-party requests** — every font, script and image is served from
   the house.
2. **No tracking** — no analytics, no behavioral inference, one cookie
   sitewide (the thinkroom's passphrase gate), explicit localStorage/
   sessionStorage keys each documented on `/closed-loop/instrumentation`.
3. **Self-hosted everything** — fonts, scripts, imagery; the static
   psychopolitics atlas vendors its own React.
4. **The voice gate** — prose in A. Selimović's name is drafted, scanned
   (de-AI), and made final only by his ruling.

Also binding: the reader's apparatus measures in memory only (no storage, no
cookie, no network) and every readout ships with its plain definition; the
instrument dies with the tab, and the disclosure names it.

## Brand Commitments

- Names: **autostimulus** (the press), **A. Selimović** (the author);
  use them as set.
- The register system: works keep their own papers, inks and voices; the
  shell serves them (the Register Rule).
- Licensing facts: texts CC BY-SA 4.0, code MIT.
- The house aesthetic runs counter to the standard AI-interface look —
  confirmed as a durable commitment (the cream/broadsheet clusters are
  retired, not a phase).

## Evidence on Hand

- **Absences future work must not fabricate:** no testimonials, no press

## Product Principles

1. The medium carries the argument; the site demonstrates what its works
   claim.
2. Truth over spectacle: every displayed quantity is measurable and defined.
3. Registers are sovereign: a work never gets flattened into the shell's
   voice; the shell never gets pulled into a work's fiction.
4. Nothing leaves the reader's machine that they did not choose.
5. The author's voice is his own: drafts are machine-made, rulings are human.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

House floor (audited): functional text ≥ 11px, WCAG AA contrast in every
register (light and dark), landmarks and keyboard focus on every surface,
reduced-motion respected by every authored moment.
