# Surface brief — / (the atelier shell) — The Observing Machine

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The site becomes a declared instance of the loop it theorizes: one honest, session-local instrument — the reader's apparatus — visible on every surface, showing the shape of being capturable (time, pauses, returns) while capturing nothing. It refuses the category default of invisible analytics, and refuses spectacle as its opposite: the machine is small, visible, and truthful.

**OWN-WORLD.** The house register (paper, ink, oxblood; EB Garamond + JetBrains Mono; hairlines, register plates). The instrument is apparatus-grade: mono readouts, numbered plain definitions, a printable offprint. No new palette; the machine wears the atelier's own print language.

**STORY.** The visitor notices a quiet line in the lower corner. Opening it, they read five plain quantities about their own session, each with a plain definition. At the offprint they see the session's shape drawn, and can print it. The framework's claims acquire a small instrument they can hold.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Existing surfaces unchanged. The instrument enters as a fixed bottom-left line (mono, small, collapsed): session time, pauses, works. Expanded: a register-plate panel with numbered readouts, definitions, and "file the offprint". On /reader the offprint leads with the reading-path diagram.

**FORM.** User-pinned direction — The Observing Machine, from a four-direction pitch, his lock 2026-10-10. Code-led (no image generation in harness); no seed key (pinned).

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
