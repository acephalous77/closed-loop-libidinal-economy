This site does not track you.

There is no analytics software running on this page. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Fathom, no self-hosted analytics. No tracking pixels, no behavioral inference, no cohort analysis, no event logging beyond what your browser tells the web server (the IP address making the request, the user-agent string, the page requested — the minimum required for HTTP to work). These server logs are kept for 7 days and then deleted.

There are no cookies. No local storage, no session storage, no IndexedDB. The site is fully static and the same for every reader.

If you write to me at the email address on the about page, your message is not logged or analyzed beyond what your email client and mine do in the normal course of mail. I do not maintain a mailing list. There is nothing to unsubscribe from.
